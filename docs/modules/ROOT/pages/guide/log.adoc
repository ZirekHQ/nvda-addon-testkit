`wait_for(pattern, *, timeout=10.0, since=None, flags=re.IGNORECASE)` searches record messages
with `re.search`. With `since=None` it snapshots `index()` at call time and only sees records
logged afterwards; pass `since=0` (as above) to search the whole session, or an `index()` taken
earlier to ignore anything logged before that point.

`index()`, `since(index)`, `all()` and `clear()` mirror xref:guide/speech.adoc[`nvda.speech`].
`errors()` and `warnings()` filter by level (`warnings()` includes errors); `assert_no_errors()`
and `assert_no_warnings()` raise `AssertionError` with the offending records attached. All four
take `since=0` to limit the search.
